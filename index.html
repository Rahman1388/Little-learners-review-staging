Safeguards for reviewers

Use adult-only test examples. Do not enter a child's name, address, school, photograph, voice recording or other identifying information. The review helpers cannot automatically anonymize a person's writing. Do not share review text without checking its contents. News pages may open official public sites only when you choose an external link.

Words are read aloud by an adult, not by built-in recorded child voices; the app does not assess oral pronunciation or fluency. Practised stages are not mastery scores. Educator review of the content and Arabic wording, and real-iPhone Safari/VoiceOver checks, are still pending.

This is a publicly accessible, independent adult-review staging site. It does not replace the existing public app, send feedback to schools or the Ministry, or certify curriculum alignment.
