# Little Learning Academy — Search Visibility & Learning Feedback Plan
*Adult planning note for future release, 28 October 2026 — no claims of rankings or approvals*

## 1. Search / App Store discoverability (no ranking guarantees)
Search positions cannot be guaranteed. A paid offer of a guaranteed #1 Google, Apple App Store, or Google Play ranking should be treated skeptically. Actual ranking depends on query intent, competition, quality, relevance, engagement, user experience, platform policies and many other changing signals. This review site is marked **noindex**; we do not intend to market this unapproved public staging preview to children.

After a professional bilingual name is legally cleared, and after teacher, Arabic, accessibility, child privacy and app-release reviews:
- Create honest English/Arabic positioning for Qatar parents; specify supplementary Grade 1–6 **aspiration**, with Grade 2 as the **current pilot**.
- Publish a public, separate parent-facing informational landing page only after its contents and privacy are approved; do not attempt SEO for draft activities.
- Use descriptive and non-misleading page titles, readable URLs, human-friendly summaries and accessible bilingual headings.
- For app stores, research platform-specific name, subtitle/short description, categories, screenshots and preview quality. Check actual Apple and Google guidelines then. Never use names or marks that have not passed trademark/domain checks.
- Track meaningful metrics only with privacy-respecting, adult-facing/consented methods. Do not deploy child behaviour analytics by default.
- Seek voluntary professional feedback from Qatar educators before making any claims about curriculum alignment.
- Avoid keyword stuffing, purchased reviews, fake downloads, misleading certification, or promises of a fixed ranking.

## 2. Children's practice feedback (not a competitive rank)
If the request for "ranking" refers to learner grades or rank, the safer draft for young children is a **private, parent/teacher-controlled progress view**, not a public leaderboard or comparison of identifiable children.
- Current adult review pilot: 8 Word Family Garden flowers are marked **tried in this visit** only, without storage or mastery claim.
- Future option after teacher review: use skill-specific outcomes (e.g. hears word ending, identifies ending, blends with guidance) and optional encouragement, not a fake "pass/fail" grade based on one multiple-choice attempt.
- Keep any individual pupil records out of a public GitHub Pages site. Before storing anything, conduct child privacy and security review, parental consent, school-policy review, and consider both Qatar requirements and applicable platform child policies.
- Teacher-generated certificates/grades, if introduced, must reflect clear reviewed criteria, not simply opening a lesson.

## 3. Audio readiness
The eight-family adult staging Explorer now uses 16 locally hosted eSpeak-generated English MP3 **whole-word** examples, with browser/device Arabic TTS as a separate optional aid. This is not professional child pronunciation audio or phoneme-by-phoneme recording. Before launch: professional native quality review, onset/rime/whole-word segmentation, fluent Arabic audio, licensing check, and physical iPhone Safari/VoiceOver audio QA.
