Use this independent page to test all 16 English sample words on an iPhone. The players below are native browser audio controls: there is no JavaScript and no quiz to unlock.

First try: tap Play under ring, then under sing. Turn up your media volume during playback. If nothing plays, copy this page's address into the standalone Safari app, not the smaller browser opened inside ChatGPT.

These are short, basic synthetic whole-word recordings for adult review. They are not child voices or expertly validated phoneme-by-phoneme blending. Arabic audio is not included on this page. Do not use as final student pronunciation material.

If tapping Play still produces no sound

  1. Open this page directly in the Safari app. An in-app viewer can behave differently.
  2. Press Play on the first native audio control, then raise iPhone media volume with the side volume buttons.
  3. Check if sound is connected to Bluetooth headphones or another output.
  4. Tap the Open ring audio directly link above. If it fails to open, the hosted clip may be inaccessible.
  5. If native audio plays here but the Word Family Garden is silent or unclickable, report that distinction: the interactive page needs a separate fix.

This page requires no microphone, login, student information, tracking or saved score. Teacher, bilingual pronunciation and real-device reviews remain pending.
