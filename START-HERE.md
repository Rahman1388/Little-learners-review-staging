## One start button, one current phonics lesson
On the homepage click **🚀 START LEARNING — Latest Version**. It opens **Picture Word Explorer + Word Family Garden** with:
- eight phonics families and sixteen picture-word examples, English + Arabic together;
- See → Match → Build → Celebrate practice, corrections, and visit-only flower marks;
- short synthetic English MP3 pronunciation clips and native Play fallback (not recorded child voices);
- clear links onward to the Grade 2 English five-module learning pilot.

**Do not begin with** the old five-word mini-lesson or original seven-stage phonics prototype. Those are intentionally hidden under **Previous experiments** on the homepage so they are preserved for teachers, not presented as competing starting points.

## If your iPhone has no sound
1. From the main START LEARNING page tap **🔊 Test sound: ring**; choosing a flower on its own does not play audio.
2. Use the blue **Hear this word** buttons under the pictures, or the built-in **Play** controls beneath the Garden.
3. If playing inside an in-app viewer is still silent, **copy the link into Safari**, increase **media volume**, and try again.
4. A simple independent **iPhone Sound Check** with native Play controls for all sixteen English clips is available from the main page’s **Previous experiments and audio troubleshooting** section, or from the Explorer navigation.

GitHub confirms the 16 MP3 files are present. This does **not** prove that sound functions on every real iPhone. Arabic sound currently depends on device speech voices and may be unavailable; ask a fluent adult to read the Arabic words when necessary.
